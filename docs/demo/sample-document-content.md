## DEMO-SOP-001 — Specimen Receipt and Acceptance
**Purpose:** Demonstrate controlled specimen-receipt documentation, exception logging, and staff training assignments.

**Scope:** Fictional receiving staff at Trace Scientific Demo Laboratory.

**Illustrative process:**
1. Log into the demo accessioning workspace using an assigned fictional account.
2. Review the synthetic specimen example and its mock identifiers.
3. Record whether the example is marked accepted, conditionally accepted, or rejected.
4. Document a fictional discrepancy and route it for supervisor review.
5. Link the demonstration form DEMO-FRM-001 as supporting evidence.

**Demo training prompt:** Explain why an exception must remain traceable and why only authorized staff may finalize decisions.

## DEMO-SOP-004 — Document Control and Revision

**Demo training prompt:** Compare the draft, approved, effective, and superseded states.

## DEMO-SOP-007 — Nonconforming Work and CAPA
**Purpose:** Demonstrate a fictional quality event, investigation, corrective action, and effectiveness follow-up.

**Scenario:** A synthetic training exercise identifies a missing equipment-log entry. No real specimen, result, or patient is involved.

**Illustrative process:**

2. Describe the fictional discrepancy and initial containment.
3. Assign a fictional event owner and target due date.

5. Demonstrate review and follow-up without claiming an actual accredited CAPA outcome.
