# Trace QMS — customer demonstration script and synthetic content plan

**Planning-only materials.** No tenant, users, documents, or live database records are created by this file. All names and records below are fictional.

## Demonstration objective (15 minutes)

Show a prospective laboratory customer how Trace QMS supports controlled documents, review and approval, revision traceability, role-based access, and audit evidence. Demonstrate only capabilities verified in the deployed build; if a feature is not enabled, describe it as planned rather than working.

| Time | Demonstration | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 min | Introduce the synthetic Trace Demo Laboratory and role-based navigation | Demo banner; synthetic organization; clearly fictional users |
| 2–5 min | Locate and open a controlled SOP; inspect revision, status and effective date | Document metadata and revision history |
| 5–8 min | Demonstrate a draft revision and route for review | Workflow steps, assigned reviewer and controlled status transitions |
| 8–10 min | Show reviewer/approver permissions and approval evidence | Role-specific actions and timestamped events; no shared credentials |
| 10–12 min | Demonstrate publication and acknowledgment/training **only if supported** | Approved document, effective revision, user acknowledgment evidence |
| 12–14 min | View audit history and demonstrate access isolation | Event actor, action, time, record; no cross-tenant records |
| 14–15 min | Questions and implementation discussion | Customer needs and next steps |

## Synthetic roles and personas

- **Demo Administrator** — organization settings and role assignments; not used for ordinary approvals.
- **Quality Manager** — controlled document owner and approval oversight.
- **Laboratory Supervisor** — SOP reviewer.
- **Laboratory Technologist** — document reader and acknowledgment participant, where supported.
- **Read-Only Auditor** — inspect released documents and audit history without editing.

Create separate synthetic accounts only after demo tenant activation has been separately approved. Use strong unique credentials and MFA where supported; never store passwords in fixtures or GitHub.

## Proposed workflow scenario

1. Quality Manager opens SOP-QC-002 v1.0 and creates a draft revision v1.1.
2. Reviewer checks the draft and records comments or requested changes.
3. Quality Manager resolves comments and routes the final revision for approval.
4. Approver signs or approves using the deployed application's supported mechanism; do not claim Part 11 compliance without independent validation.
5. Published revision becomes visible to authorized readers, with history preserved.
6. Auditor views corresponding audit events and confirms an ordinary reader cannot edit or approve.

**No fabricated audit entries:** audit evidence must be generated by actual demo interactions once separately authorized.
